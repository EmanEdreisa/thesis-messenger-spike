Privacy Policy

How this Messenger app handles data during the thesis study.

What this is

This app is a research prototype for a BS Computer Engineering thesis at De La Salle University. It is being tested with one partner food business, which uses it to take pickup orders through its Facebook Page. It is not a commercial product.

What we collect

When a customer messages the Page, we save the text of their message and a masked identifier for who sent it. We use this to process pickup orders and to evaluate how well the prototype works for the thesis.

Facebook permissions we use

  • pages_messaging - to receive and reply to customer messages
  • pages_manage_posts - to publish promotional posts to the Page
  • pages_read_engagement - to read the Page's own info and posts
  • pages_show_list - to let the Page owner connect their Page to this app

What we don't collect

We do not store customer names, profile pictures, payment screenshots, or GCash details. The sender identifier we save is masked, not a customer's real Facebook ID.

How long we keep it

Data collected during this study is deleted six months after the thesis is approved.
